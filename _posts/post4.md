It has been a long time since I've written one of these blog posts, essentially journal entries. It feels like I've grown a lot but also I haven't moved.

I work on my self as much as I can every day. I want to be the best person I can be, but I find I don't fully have a grasp on what that entails. Who do I want to be, what do I aspire towards. The answer seems hazy, ever-changing, or otherwise inanswerable. I suppose if it were concrete there would come a point where growth would cease.

Are these questions pointless? Sometimes it feels like it. They don't point me in any direction, quite the contrary, they keep me in place pondering the vastness of the human experience. Are these questions others have too?

I've been slowly gathering a greater appreciation for artistry. I think I want to feel. I'll end this blog post here.
